Practical info

Everything you need to plan

  • Start point: Koggala Lake pier (exact pin after booking)
  • Best times: Early morning or late afternoon
  • What to bring: Hat, sunscreen, water, camera
  • What is included: Boat, guide, life jackets
  • Not included: Meals, hotel pickup (available as add-on)

Safety and respect

Calm waters and experienced local guides make the ride easy for all ages. Please avoid littering, keep noise low near wildlife, and follow guide instructions.

FAQ

Helpful answers before you book

Is it private?

Yes. Most tours are private, and we can arrange shared options on request.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. Calm waters, life jackets, and a steady pace make it family friendly.

Can we spot crocodiles?

Wildlife is unpredictable. Sightings are rare, and we keep a respectful distance.

What if it rains?

Light rain is usually fine. Heavy rain may require rescheduling or a refund.

How do we pay?

Reserve by message. Pay in cash or via agreed online options at confirmation.
